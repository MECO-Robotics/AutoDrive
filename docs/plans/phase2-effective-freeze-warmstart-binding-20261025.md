## Bounded implementation scope

Implement one versioned, fail-closed binding path through the existing multi-case APIs:

1. Add required, separately typed production inputs to a new descriptor schema (or strictly versioned extension): (a) effective Phase 0 materialization; (b) the human score-estimand approval artifact; and (c) an explicit crosswalk from the multi-case requests to Phase 0 tuple IDs. On describe and every revalidation, call `validate_effective_phase0_bundle` against the same clean source root and call `meco_simulated_score_ledger.load_estimand_approval(path, source_identity_sha256=...)`. Preserve the exact canonical approval bytes and bind their SHA-256, approval ID, source identity, rules ID and rules digest. The ledger API's `estimand_approval_digest` / `validate_estimand_approval` are the authority; do not accept a caller-supplied “approved” boolean or digest alone. Reject missing, legacy, stale, source-mismatched, internally inconsistent, or noncanonical inputs. Older descriptors remain explicitly legacy diagnostic only.
2. Define the Phase 0 tuple crosswalk as a closed relation, not a scenario/seed guess. For every requested `scenario_id/category/seeds` case, map to exact Phase 0 `tuple_id`s and retain each tuple's full identity: `scenario_id`, `case_id`, `category`, `defender_family`, `layout_id`, `start_condition`, `sensor_profile`, `seed`, `repetition`, and `evaluation_phase`. Recompute tuple IDs with `meco_baseline_freeze.canonical_sha256` and require exact equality to tuple registry entries. Each mapped tuple must be eligible and assigned to `train`; every Phase 0 train tuple ID must appear exactly once, and no calibration, validation, or final-test tuple may appear. Case keys, seed lists, case IDs and all axes must agree across crosswalk, descriptor and protocol. Recompute this crosswalk from the validated Phase 0 bundle on initial descriptor construction and every resume, combine, fit, bundle validation, and manifest validation. Incomplete closure, duplicate IDs, unknown IDs, altered axes or any split overlap fails closed. This means a requested multicase subset is partial and cannot be represented as the production full-train bundle.
3. Keep three authorities separate in descriptor and artifacts: (i) the Phase 0 tuple allocation authorization establishes the permitted tuple-to-partition assignment and is validated by `validate_effective_phase0_bundle`; (ii) the score-estimand approval establishes only the approved simulator score outcome/rules/source and is validated through `meco_simulated_score_ledger`; (iii) target-label estimands establish what U, B, P, and C mean. The score approval does not approve the learning labels, and the label contracts do not grant allocation authority. Record separate schema/version and digests for each. Bind the score approval bytes/digest, source/rules identity, Phase 0 freeze/partition, and exact tuple crosswalk through every shard receipt, each `trajectory`/`ub`/`p` sidecar, combined family manifest, all five fitted weights, validator receipt, final bundle, and training-manifest export.
4. Keep the existing target consumers and validators. A1/A2/A3 must each fit from the same source-bound U+B data and separate independently labeled P-route data. U is the declared bounded sampled legal-node gross-return target; B is only a feasible supported behavior preference from valid comparable records or a separately declared human annotation source bound to its authority digest; P is only the independent oracle-v3 executed-route target. Give each label contract its own explicit version, target definition, supporting source evidence, coverage denominator, and censoring/tie handling. Never derive U/B/P from a teacher action, aggregate score alone, missing/censored outcome, or route-cost demonstration treated as optimal.
5. C1/C2 must fit from the same source-bound candidate rows, candidate IDs/order/masks, route-cost and safety constraints, and repeated matched gross-horizon returns/pair labels. Preserve repeat-level values, paired seeds, tie/variance/censoring information, and abstain on unsupported or unstable comparisons. Declare C gross-return/choice labels independently of the approved score ledger (which may be one recorded outcome component), keep semantics shared with D1, and do not subtract route cost twice.
6. Have `validate_multicase_warm_start_bundle` reopen and revalidate the Phase 0 bundle, exact score approval bytes via the ledger validator, complete tuple crosswalk/train closure, combined sidecars, all five track-specific weights, and every receipt before export. Keep `phase2_ready`, `phase2_complete`, and `qualification_eligible` false unless separate authoritative validators establish their precise gates. The training manifest must preserve descriptor/bundle/freeze/partition/approval/tuple-crosswalk/label-contract digests alongside the exact five artifact paths and hashes.

## Fairness and source constraints

- Use one clean reviewed source tree, exact frozen protocol bytes, and the same declared train cases/seeds, partition, scenario/opponent allocation, and evaluation budget for all five tracks. Do not admit validation, final-test, or calibration tuples into collection or fitting.
- Require complete Phase 0 eligible train tuple-ID closure across every axis, and exact crosswalk closure to protocol case/seed requests. The same tuple membership and evaluation budget must hold for A1/A2/A3/C1/C2. Reject any calibration/validation/final-test tuple at every row and artifact boundary. Do not pool attempts with different descriptors, source trees, protocol bytes, runtime contracts, devices, approvals, tuple registries, or partitions.
- Keep the score-approval artifact immutable and source/rules bound. It only authorizes the declared simulated score outcome; it does not establish target-label quality, expert optimality, broader official score semantics, or final-test access. Allocation authorization remains the separately pinned Phase 0 preregistration artifact.
- C1/C2 use the same deterministic candidate generator and exact candidate table, ordering, feasibility/safety masks, K, route estimates, input information, matched rollout seeds, and reward horizon. D1 must use that same candidate table and route/risk estimates. Record candidate recall as a separate future evaluation requirement; the ranker cannot select omitted candidates.
- A1/A2/A3 share the same legal support, node/behavior sampling declaration, U/B/P target definitions, source corpus, and P oracle. Report per-track/per-case target coverage and denominators. P0 or U+B-only weights are diagnostic, not the required full five-track Phase 2 warm-start set.
- Record actual indexed device identity under the lease (physical WX9100 model, BDF, vendor/device, HIP UUID, inventory digest), not only `cuda:N`. Host-side serialization may use CPU; simulator physics/routing and model fitting must pass the project's GPU-only gates without silent CPU fallback.

## Required contract coverage

After implementation, focused metadata/contract tests must establish:

- absent, altered, stale, wrong-source, wrong-partition, or unapproved Phase 0 materialization is rejected at descriptor, receipt, combined dataset, artifact, bundle, and training-manifest boundaries;
- missing, noncanonical, altered, wrong-source, wrong-plan, wrong-rules, or semantically unsupported human score-estimand approval bytes fail through `meco_simulated_score_ledger.load_estimand_approval` / `validate_estimand_approval`; its exact approval digest, rule ID/digest and source binding are preserved at every production layer;
- tuple crosswalk rejects any tuple ID whose recomputed canonical ID or full-axis record differs, duplicate or unreferenced train IDs, incomplete train closure, mismatched case IDs/seeds, and any calibration/validation/final-test overlap, including on resume and final manifest revalidation;
- edits to dataset, sidecar, receipt, descriptor, bundle, or any of five weights bytes fail digest revalidation; mixed attempts and source/device/runtime identities fail;
- omitted/duplicated/reordered cases, seeds, target families, legal support, candidate rows, repeats, P branches, or track artifacts fail; validation/final-test seeds fail even when rows otherwise look valid;
- A artifacts cannot pass the full U+B+P gate with missing, censored, unsupported, p0, or non-oracle P targets; C1/C2 reject mismatched candidate tables, unpaired repeats/seeds, manufactured ties/preferences, and unsupported gross-return targets;
- the exact five-track path/hash set and freeze/partition/estimand binding survive bundle export and supervisor initialization metadata; all readiness and qualification claims remain false absent their independent gates.

Tests that exercise simulated game behavior require a representative seeded end-to-end simulator evaluation and inspected fresh replay. Before generating/reviewing that replay, restart and verify the dashboard on the current checkout, including backend endpoints and frontend asset identity, per `AGENTS.md`. Do not count metadata tests or prefix-replay integrity alone as behavior validation.
