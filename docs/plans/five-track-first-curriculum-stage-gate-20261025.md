## Objective

Keep the live r4 diagnostic campaign unchanged and training uninterrupted. This is a per-policy curriculum milestone. It does not establish the full learning-plan primary claim in `docs/MECO_AutoDrive_Learning_Tracks_Evaluation_Plan.md` §6 (beating the strongest matched deterministic baseline on held-out match outcomes with confidence intervals and no safety increase).

## Authoritative distinction: exposure versus qualification

The scheduler's `pickup_simple` exposure progression is not a qualification result:

- `frc_defense/meco_task_scheduler.py` defaults to 10 exposure chunks per scenario. It treats a scenario as exposure-complete only after the chunk count is reached and each required category has appeared. This unlocks scheduler progression.
- Scheduler assignments report `promotion_status: "not_evaluated_by_exposure_scheduler"`. Exposure state is scheduling state; it does not establish task success or set persistent qualification state.
- Therefore 10/10 exposure chunks alone are insufficient. The required `single` and `cluster` appearances are also needed for scheduler unlock, and neither condition constitutes a stage pass.
- At the observed r4 status timestamp `2026-10-25T17:42:03Z`, r4 had 5/10 `pickup_simple` chunks (4 cluster, 1 single). Its current assignment was diagnostic `stage_exposure`, with promotion explicitly unevaluated.

The formal first-stage promotion gate is defined by `frc_defense/training_curriculum.py` for `pickup_simple`:

- Evaluate the required categories `single` and `cluster` independently.
- Each validation round must cover every case row and seed in the frozen v4 case table, with at least 100 episodes per category in each frozen validation stratum. The curriculum gate requires at least 90% success for each required category (`single`, `cluster`) aggregated across that category's cases. The v4 validator's exact case-table and seed-coverage checks apply; it computes category-level useful-throughput and safety confidence intervals by the frozen protocol's cluster bootstrap over complete strata, resampling `(layout_id, defender_family)` clusters. `pickup_simple` has no additional throughput-ratio or absolute useful-throughput threshold in its primitive curriculum definition.
- Require three consecutive passing validation rounds for each policy. Reports must meet the evaluator's split, protocol, checkpoint, category, frozen-stratum, exact-seed, and binding checks; a failure resets the consecutive-pass streak.
- Track this state separately for A1, A2, A3, C1, and C2. No aggregate five-track result may conceal an individual policy failure.
- When using the current qualification pipeline, also satisfy its calibrated v4 useful-throughput and safety checks against the frozen reference and D0 floor. The primitive curriculum threshold does not waive those qualification checks.

“Pass first stage” therefore means that one named policy has a complete, auditable, three-round formal promotion record under one immutable, source-bound, calibrated v4 protocol, with every frozen case and seed covered, the required per-category/per-stratum episode counts, category-aggregate success rates, category-level reference-margin and safety results from the protocol's cluster bootstrap, and checkpoint/report provenance. The five-track milestone is achieved only when all five named policies independently satisfy that definition. Scheduler exposure completion is a prerequisite for progression through the training curriculum, not the qualification evidence itself.

## Current state and blockers

Its manifest states `qualification_eligible=false`; live status reports diagnostic split and `evaluation_binding=null`. It uses the provisional `meco-qualification-v1` calibration, which has not passed baseline variance review and is nonpromotable. No evaluation protocol is bound. At the external supervisor-status snapshot `2026-10-25T17:45:34Z`, the queue was lease-aware round-robin: A1 and A2 were running on `cuda:0` and `cuda:1`; A3, C1, and C2 were queued; all lanes showed 500 completed updates except A2 at 510. All five warm-start artifacts were absent, and phase-two, validation, and qualification readiness were false. The active scheduler remained at 5/10 exposure chunks (4 cluster, 1 single).

Consequently, r4 training updates and diagnostic evaluations cannot count as formal stage-one validation reports. Do not edit its manifest, scheduler state, process, leases, or output tree; do not relabel its diagnostic results or attach a qualification binding after the fact. Continue the currently authorized diagnostic training until the user manually stops it. After that single user-stop handoff and confirmed lease release, preserve immutable snapshots of each latest r4 checkpoint and its exact source, runtime/build, model architecture, track config, and training provenance. A checkpoint may be used as the candidate for a fresh, read-only v4 qualification evaluation only if the frozen source/runtime/model/config identities match exactly and the v4 validator accepts the checkpoint binding. Diagnostic report data remains excluded. If the identity check fails, record the exact mismatched fields and obtain separate review before deciding whether compatible retraining is necessary; do not assume a from-scratch restart. Do not launch a replacement or qualifying run as part of this plan.

The existing `pickup_simple` D0 calibration is tied to an older source identity and is historical D0-only evidence. It cannot be reused as current-source calibration. The repository also lacks a completed matching source freeze/partition capture, reviewed matched-reference variance and margins, and calibrated v4 protocol. The absent Phase 2 warm-start bundle blocks starting new Phase 2 training, but does not alone require discarding already-trained r4 checkpoints: snapshot and exact identity compatibility must be assessed first. The detailed protocol and readiness preconditions remain governed by `docs/MECO_QUALIFICATION.md`, `docs/plans/phase0-baseline-heldout-calibration-20261025.md`, and `docs/MECO_NEXT_FIVE_TRACK_TRAINING_HANDOFF.md`.

`docs/MECO_QUALIFICATION.md` also records that existing evaluation has host-side compute. Therefore device dispatch or a no-fallback flag alone is not sufficient for the GPU-only constraint: the qualifying path must demonstrate GPU residency for all numerical/algorithmic work, including training, candidate generation/scoring, route/risk computation, and statistical evaluation. CPU is restricted to non-compute orchestration and I/O only where allowed by the user's explicit GPU-only requirement.

## Acceptance evidence

The five-track first-stage milestone is accepted only with:

- Five independently complete promotion records, one each for A1, A2, A3, C1, and C2, each showing three consecutive passing `pickup_simple` validation rounds under the same calibrated v4 protocol.
- For each round and policy: every frozen case-table row and seed covered; at least 100 evaluated episodes in each of `single` and `cluster` in every frozen stratum; at least 90% success aggregated across the cases for each category; complete bound report IDs and checkpoint identities; and passing category-level v4 useful-throughput and safety confidence intervals computed with the frozen protocol's cluster bootstrap over complete strata.
- Source, runtime/build, simulator, protocol, reference, D0, partition, seed-set, and bundle digests matching the frozen manifest and reports.
- No diagnostic or provisional-v1 artifacts counted; no exposure counter used as qualification evidence; no track result substituted for another.
- Simulator records/replays inspected for representative seeded cases, with current dashboard backend and frontend checks recorded.
- Live post-gate evidence that training resumes from each compatible latest checkpoint and continues across all five lanes with non-starved lease-aware progression, all required leases respected on the two WX9100s, GPU-resident algorithmic/numerical training, candidate, route/risk, and statistical evaluation compute, and no lane stopped by evaluation.

This acceptance is the first curriculum-stage qualification only. The full plan's held-out match-outcome architecture comparison remains a separate milestone.
